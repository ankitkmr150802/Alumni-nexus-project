FAQs

How do you keep alumni data accurate?

We use deduplication, verification prompts, and optional periodic check-ins to keep profiles up-to-date.

Can students access the platform?

Yes, you can enable student access for mentorship matching, events, and networking, controlled by admin permissions.

Is our data secure?

We follow best practices for encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access, and detailed audit logs.

Do you help with onboarding?

Absolutely. We offer data import assistance, training sessions, and dedicated support.
